About

Booking systems are my specialty

I'm a software engineer focused on WordPress & WooCommerce, and a core developer behind the MagePeople Team plugin suite — a family of booking and rental products for bus, tour, taxi, event, ferry, rental, service and theater businesses, along with dozens of add-ons and WooCommerce extensions. I build clean, self-contained plugin architectures (custom MVC, routers, Active-Record models) and care about security, escaping, and WordPress coding standards. Beyond WordPress I also ship full-stack apps in React / TypeScript, Laravel and Flutter.
